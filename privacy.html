Eggling – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the Eggling game ("Eggling", "the app") collects and how it is used. Eggling is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. Eggling has no accounts or sign-in, and we run no servers that receive your data. Your game progress is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Eggling shows ads using Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID
  • Approximate location, estimated from your IP address
  • Interactions with ads, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the app and ads

This data is collected and handled by Google under its own policies. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Where required by law, such as in the EEA and UK, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • You can remove all game data stored on your device by uninstalling the app.

Children

Eggling is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
