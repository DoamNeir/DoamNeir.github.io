Birdex - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Short version: Birdex has no accounts and we run no servers. Your sightings, photos and settings stay on your phone and are never sent to us. The iPhone version has no ads, analytics or tracking. The free Android version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect device and usage data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

This policy covers Birdex for iPhone and for Android.

What the app stores on your phone

Your sightings (which bird, date, place and notes), the photos you add, your home region and your settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time in Profile, Settings, "Delete all sightings".

Location

Birdex asks for your location only when you tap "Use my current location" while logging a sighting. The location is saved with that sighting on your device and used to show birds you could find nearby. To turn a location into a place name, the app uses the phone's built-in geocoding service: Apple's on iPhone, covered by Apple's Privacy Policy, and Google's on Android, covered by Google's Privacy Policy. You can change location access at any time in your phone's settings.

Camera and photos

If you choose to, you can take a photo or pick one from your library to add to a sighting. Birdex reads the date and place stored in that photo to fill in the sighting for you. The photo is saved inside the app on your device and is never uploaded. Birdex only sees the photos you pick.

Advertising (Android version only)

Birdex for Android shows banner ads served by Google AdMob, in their own space on the screen. To deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID.
  • Information about ad views and taps, and app diagnostics.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.

Your sightings, photos and the locations you save with them are never shared with Google or advertisers. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time in Profile, Settings, "Privacy choices". If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no collection of your name, email or contacts.
  • No analytics of our own, and no ads at all on iPhone.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No selling of personal information.

Children

Birdex is made for a general audience. The Android version, which shows ads, is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
