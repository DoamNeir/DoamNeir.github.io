Birdex - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Short version: Birdex does not collect any data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we run no servers. Your sightings, photos and settings stay on your iPhone.

What the app stores on your iPhone

Your sightings (which bird, date, place and notes), the photos you add, your home region and your settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time in Profile, Settings, "Delete all sightings".

Location

Birdex asks for your location only when you tap "Use my current location" while logging a sighting. The location is saved with that sighting on your device and used to show birds you could find nearby. To turn a location into a place name, the app uses Apple's built-in geocoding service, which is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can change location access at any time in iPhone Settings.

Camera and photos

If you choose to, you can take a photo or pick one from your library to add to a sighting. Birdex reads the date and place stored in that photo to fill in the sighting for you. The photo is saved inside the app on your device and is never uploaded. Birdex only sees the photos you pick.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no collection of your name, email or contacts.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Children

Birdex is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
