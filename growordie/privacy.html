Grow or Die - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Short version: Grow or Die does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What the game stores

Your progress (DNA, unlocked worlds and mutations, missions, saved species and settings) is stored only on your device. It never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no collection of names, email addresses, contacts, photos, location or device identifiers.
  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No in-app purchases.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
