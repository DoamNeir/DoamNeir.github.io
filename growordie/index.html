Grow or Die - Support

Start as a tiny organism. Eat. Survive. Evolve. Become something different every run.

Need help or found a bug? Email daniomerdani@gmail.com and tell us your phone model and what happened.

How do I move?

Put your thumb anywhere on the screen and drag. Your creature swims the way you drag. Double-tap to dash once you evolve Jet Pores.

What can I eat?

Anything with a faint white ring is smaller than you, so eat it. A red ring means it can hurt you, so swim away.

How do I unlock new worlds?

Reach the final stage of a world, defeat or survive its last boss, and choose Escape. That discovers the next world: buy it in the Shop with DNA, then switch worlds with the arrows beside the Evolve button.

What do skins do?

Nothing to the gameplay: skins only change how your creature looks. Starting organisms in the Shop are different: they begin a run with a mutation.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress. See the privacy policy.
